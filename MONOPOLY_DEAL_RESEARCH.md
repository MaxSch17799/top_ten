# Monopoly Deal rules and source review

Research date: 2026-10-30. This report distinguishes editions because Hasbro's live FAQ and its newer printed instructions disagree.

## Recommended rules basis

The implementation's default **Classic** uses the E3113 basic game and Hasbro FAQ interpretation: reactive Just Say No cards do not consume turn plays. A separate **2024 printed rules** profile should charge the current player one play for their own Just Say No, while opponents' responses remain free. Both profiles can use the newer instructions' explicit building clarifications. Label this choice in lobby descriptions rather than describing every historical ruling as universally official.

B0965 lists 110 cards including four reference cards, therefore **106 playable cards**:

| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Properties: 28     | Brown 2; light blue 3; pink 3; orange 3; red 3; yellow 3; green 3; dark blue 2; railroads 4; utilities 2                                     |
| Property wilds: 11 | Brown/light blue 1; light blue/railroad 1; pink/orange 2; red/yellow 2; dark blue/green 1; green/railroad 1; railroad/utility 1; any-color 2 |
| Actions: 34        | Deal Breaker 2; Forced Deal 3; Sly Deal 3; Just Say No 3; Debt Collector 3; Birthday 3; Double Rent 2; House 3; Hotel 2; Pass Go 10          |
| Rent: 13           | Two of each of five pairs: brown/light blue, pink/orange, red/yellow, dark blue/green, railroad/utility; three any-color rents               |
| Money: 20          | 1M ×6; 2M ×5; 3M ×3; 4M ×3; 5M ×2; 10M ×1                                                                                                    |

Wild Rent is worth **3M**; paired rent is worth **1M**. Neither type changes the standard color-set rent schedule.

## Interactions that affect engine design

- **Winning and turn structure:** E3113 ends the game when a player has three complete sets of different colors. It deals five, draws two (five if the turn starts empty), permits zero through three plays, and discards to seven. A payment or Forced Deal recipient may therefore win immediately; the old 2008 own-turn declaration delay is absent from E3113 and G0351. Immediate off-turn detection is the implementation interpretation of those newer instructions.
- **Rearrangement:** [FAQ 922](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/922) and [FAQ 937](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/937) allow ordinary properties and wilds to move between the owner's groups freely, only during their turn. Fixed properties keep their color; dual wilds use their printed colors; rainbows use any property color. G0351 prevents overfilling a group and expressly permits starting another one. Model groups as an array, not one storage slot per color. Extra same-color complete sets do not provide a different winning color.
- **Wild-only groups:** G0351 forbids completing a group entirely with every-color wilds. A dual-color wild supplies a valid non-rainbow anchor. [FAQ 942](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/942) is internally inconsistent: its first sentence asks for a standard property, but its next sentence explicitly allows a dual wild with rainbows. The current printed wording supports allowing dual-wild-only completion, while forbidding rainbow-only completion.
- **Payments:** [FAQ 944](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/944) lets the payer choose bank assets, properties, or both; hand cards are ineligible. [FAQ 939](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/939) explicitly excludes the two zero-value wilds, including when insolvent. No change. Banked actions remain money for every later owner ([FAQ 943](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/943)).
- **Buildings:** The 2024 guide explicitly discards attached buildings if their group becomes incomplete. Buildings can be paid as assets and then enter the recipient's bank. Hotels need a house first; at most one of each; no railroad/utility buildings. E3113 explicitly adds house +3 and hotel +4, cumulatively +7. Apply transfers atomically before checking whether a group broke, so a swap that leaves a group complete does not discard its buildings.
- **Stealing:** [FAQ 926](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/926) protects complete groups from Sly Deal/Forced Deal and limits Deal Breaker to a complete group. [FAQ 928](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/928) includes buildings in Deal Breaker. Rainbow wilds remain eligible for steal/swap despite their zero payment value. The modern Forced Deal card specifically restricts taking from an opponent's complete group; older wording is less precise about voluntarily offering one's own completed-set card, so document any stricter both-sides restriction.
- **Rent:** [FAQ 950](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/950) charges every opponent with paired rent and exactly one chosen opponent with any-color rent. [FAQ 949](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/949) allows doubling either rent type. [FAQ 951](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/951) permits two doubles plus rent for three plays and ×4. Double cards do not affect Birthday or Debt Collector.
- **Just Say No:** [FAQ 952](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/952/~/monopoly-deal-%E2%80%93-can-i-just-say-no-to-a-just-say-no-card%3F) allows counter-counter chains. [FAQ 953](https://hasbro-new.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/953) makes responses free, whereas G0351 charges own-turn responses. Preventing a G0351 own-turn counter after the budget is exhausted follows from its three-card limit; the guide does not separately answer that edge case. G0351 explicitly says a No protects only its user; global attacks require independent response chains. Treat rent plus simultaneous doubles as one demand for cancellation; Hasbro social-channel answers conflict on this, so the choice should be documented rather than claimed undisputed.

Recommendation: retain the existing site's Cloudflare transport, lobbies, player tokens and storage. Extract only code that survives a documented classic-rules audit; otherwise write a compact project-native deterministic rules core and use the reviewed upstream tests as behavioral inspiration. Do not copy Hasbro artwork. Any copied substantial upstream code or tests must retain its full MIT notice and provenance. Merely evaluating repositories does not imply code reuse.

The upstream test counts above are inventories/README claims, not test runs performed for this research. Application build, browser and integration verification belong to the main implementation task.
